# Single-person shipboard transporter

Approved 2026-09-24: a Star Trek-inspired open transporter station, one block
wide/deep and three blocks high. This is an original shipboard asset using the
project's graphite, titanium, restrained cyan-white emission and small warm
amber/lilac instrument accents.

- Corresponding lower standing disc and overhead emitter, connected by a narrow
  rear spine. Front and sides remain open; no enclosing door.
- Chamfered base with a circular segmented emitter ring. Deck at 9/16 block;
  canopy underside above standing player headroom, within the third block.
- Recessed rear instruments and two narrow vertical light channels.
- Actual three-part occupancy, facing, collision and picking. Breaking any part
  removes the assembly and gives at most one item. Block ID remains teleporter.
- Existing placements acquire upper parts only in free space; never overwrite
  unrelated blocks. The archived authored ship blueprint is preserved.
- Existing menu, naming, core-online authority and destinations remain in place.
  Arrival uses the actual standing surface and refuses blocked transporter bays.
- Brief blue-white emitter flashes, vertical light filaments and sparkling
  particles follow successful travel. No new charge delay or player invisibility.
- Blockbench MCP authors/exports the editable mesh and textures; runtime uses
  the existing OBJ loader, split into three height-local models and full item.
